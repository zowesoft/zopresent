# Spoken script for the confidence calibration set.
#
# One clip per line:
#   <written references> :: <sentence to read aloud> [:: <type> [established=<ref>]]
# Leave the reference empty for a clip that must produce nothing. type is
# explicit (the default), contextual, quote or correction; a contextual clip
# declares the passage already on the wall, and a correction's gold is the
# passage the queue should keep after the speaker takes the first one back.
#
# Read the sentence the way it would be said from the pulpit: no reading the
# punctuation, no pausing to announce "colon". The point is to capture the
# spoken forms the detector has to score (chapter/verse in words, connectors,
# bare numbers), through a real mic, with the room's own noise.
#
# Keep clips short (one or two sentences) so the sweep has many independent
# detections; add more lines rather than longer ones.

# Explicit references: the passage is named outright.
Romans 8:28 :: Let us turn this morning to Romans chapter eight, verse twenty eight. And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God.
2 Corinthians 5:17 :: Our text is second Corinthians, chapter five, verse seventeen. Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature.
Hebrews 4:12; 2 Timothy 3:16 :: Turn with me to Hebrews chapter four verse twelve, and then second Timothy chapter three verse sixteen.
:: Before we open the word, a few announcements. The youth group meets on the twenty eighth at six thirty, and the food pantry served one hundred nineteen families last month.
:: Please stand as the band leads us in worship.

# Quotations: scripture read word for word without naming the passage.
John 3:16 :: For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish. :: quote
Psalms 51:10 :: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me. :: quote
Matthew 11:28 :: Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. :: quote
1 Peter 5:7 :: Casting all your care upon him, for he careth for you. :: quote
James 1:5 :: If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally. :: quote
Revelation 22:21 :: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen. :: quote
Genesis 1:1 :: In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. :: quote
Isaiah 40:31 :: They that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles. :: quote
Psalms 23:1-2 :: The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters. :: quote
:: The food pantry served one hundred nineteen families last month, and the Christmas program is on the twenty eighth. Come hungry, and bring a friend. :: quote

# Contextual references: a bare verse call against the passage on the wall.
John 3:29 :: Now read verse twenty nine with me. That is where the promise lands tonight. :: contextual established=John 3
Psalms 23:4 :: Let us go back to verse four for a moment. That is the verse that carries the whole psalm. :: contextual established=Psalms 23
:: The choir still needs fifteen volunteers for the Christmas program, and the youth group meets on the twenty eighth. :: contextual established=Romans 8

# Corrections: the speaker takes a reference back and names the one they meant.
Romans 8:29 :: Let us read Romans chapter eight verse twenty eight. No wait, I mean Romans chapter eight verse twenty nine. :: correction
John 3:17 :: Turn to John chapter three verse sixteen. Scratch that, let us go to John chapter three verse seventeen instead. :: correction
Romans 8:28 :: Actually, I mean, let us turn to Romans chapter eight verse twenty eight. That is the promise we are resting on tonight. :: correction
